# Campaigns

Copy [campaign-template](campaign-template.md) and [run manifest](run-manifest.example.json) into ignored lab/runs/<run-id>/ when implementing a campaign. [Case matrix](case-matrix.md) is the initial backlog, not passed coverage.

Inventory defines scope; the future runner must enforce it. Store raw scanner/capture output privately. Review findings separately and publish sanitized extracts with provenance. A completed campaign may legitimately have no confirmed vulnerabilities.
